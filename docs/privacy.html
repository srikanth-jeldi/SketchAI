Sketluma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Applies to Sketluma 0.6.4, package com.epitomehub.sketluma, by Epitomehub technologies Pvt Ltd.

Sketluma is a drawing and colouring app for children and adults. Drawing and local saving work offline. It does not require an account or include cloud artwork storage or a runtime AI service.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android store build uses Google AdMob for banner ads and interstitial ads after completing artwork. Debug builds and the separately labelled test APK use Google demo ad units. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the device IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostics and performance information, and device identifiers such as app set ID for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit. The Android build excludes Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions. Requests use child age treatment, a general-audience content rating and non-personalized-ad settings for all users, including the Adults collection; selecting an artwork category does not verify age. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosure.

Google's User Messaging Platform updates privacy requirements before the app initializes advertising or requests ads. It uses protective under-age settings for all users. Required forms are handled by Google; an “Ad privacy choices” button appears in About when the SDK requires that entry point. If privacy checks fail or permission is unavailable, advertising stays disabled and drawing remains available. Google may retain SDK data under its own policies; Sketluma does not operate an account or server-side artwork service or offer deletion of Google's records. Contact us for privacy questions. The app does not automatically upload drawings or its local diagnostic files to Google.

Your artwork

Drawings, colouring progress and preview images are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Sketluma does not automatically transmit them to a server. Exporting or sharing a picture uses your device's share sheet and the destination you choose. That destination has its own privacy practices.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps a small rotating device-local diagnostic log to help investigate failures. Records can include error messages, stack traces, timestamps, memory-pressure events and, on supported Android versions, the previous app-process exit reason, device model and Android API level. The log does not intentionally include artwork contents. Sketluma does not automatically upload it. The “Share local diagnostics” action creates an export for you to share voluntarily with a destination you choose.

Children

The Kids collection can be used offline without accounts, chat or purchases. Advertising is configured with the same child-restricted settings throughout the app, including the Adults art collection. A parent or guardian can help a child use system export/share features. The app does not request access to contacts, GPS/location, camera or microphone. The ad SDK's IP-based general-location processing described above does not require a GPS permission.

Retention and deletion

Artwork stays on the device until you delete it in the app, clear app data or uninstall. Android app backup is disabled for this build. Export work you wish to keep before uninstalling. Local diagnostic logs rotate when they reach approximately 64KiB and are also removed by clearing app data or uninstalling.

Platform services

Your operating system, Google Play and any share destination may process information under their own settings and policies. Sketluma does not control these independent services.

Contact and changes

For support or privacy questions, contact Epitomehub technologies Pvt Ltd at contactus@epitomehub.com or use the Sketluma project repository. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
