Sketluma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Applies to Sketluma 0.6.1 testing builds.

Sketluma is a drawing and colouring app for children and adults. Drawing and local saving work offline. This testing build includes Google Mobile Ads using Google's demo ad units when an internet connection is available. It does not require an account or include cloud artwork storage or a runtime AI service.

Advertising in this test build

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may contact Google to load demo banner and interstitial ads and process information such as IP address, device information, SDK diagnostics and ad interactions under Google's policies. These test units are not linked to a Sketluma monetization account. Requests are marked with child age treatment, a general-audience content rating and non-personalized-ad settings for all users. The Android build excludes Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions. These settings do not mean the SDK has no data processing. See Google's Privacy Policy. The app does not automatically upload your drawings or local diagnostic files to Google. Live ads are not enabled; data practices and any required consent flow must be reviewed before a production advertising release.

Your artwork

Drawings, colouring progress and preview images are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Sketluma does not automatically transmit them to a server. Exporting or sharing a picture uses your device's share sheet and the destination you choose. That destination has its own privacy practices.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps a small rotating device-local diagnostic log to help investigate failures. Records can include error messages, stack traces, timestamps, memory-pressure events and, on supported Android versions, the previous app-process exit reason, device model and Android API level. The log does not intentionally include artwork contents. Sketluma does not automatically upload it. The “Share local diagnostics” action creates an export for you to share voluntarily with a destination you choose.

Children

The Kids collection can be used offline without accounts, chat or purchases. Test advertising is configured with the same child-restricted settings throughout the app, including the Adults art collection. A parent or guardian can help a child use system export/share features. The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Retention and deletion

Artwork stays on the device until you delete it in the app, clear app data or uninstall. Android app backup is disabled for this build. Export work you wish to keep before uninstalling. Local diagnostic logs rotate when they reach approximately 64KiB and are also removed by clearing app data or uninstalling.

Platform services

Your operating system, Google Play and any share destination may process information under their own settings and policies. Sketluma does not control these independent services.

Contact and changes

For support, use the developer contact shown in the Sketluma Play Store listing or the Sketluma project repository. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
