Sketchora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Applies to Sketchora 0.5.0.

Sketchora is an offline drawing and colouring app for children and adults. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising, analytics service, cloud storage or runtime AI service.

Your artwork

Drawings, colouring progress and preview images are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Sketchora does not automatically transmit them to a server. Exporting or sharing a picture uses your device's share sheet and the destination you choose. That destination has its own privacy practices.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps a small rotating device-local diagnostic log to help investigate failures. Records can include error messages, stack traces, timestamps, memory-pressure events and, on supported Android versions, the previous app-process exit reason, device model and Android API level. The log does not intentionally include artwork contents. Sketchora does not automatically upload it. The “Share local diagnostics” action creates an export for you to share voluntarily with a destination you choose.

Children

The Kids collection works offline without accounts, advertising, chat, purchases or automatic data uploads. A parent or guardian can help a child use system export/share features. The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Artwork stays on the device until you delete it in the app, clear app data or uninstall. Android app backup is disabled for this build. Export work you wish to keep before uninstalling. Local diagnostic logs rotate when they reach approximately 64KiB and are also removed by clearing app data or uninstalling.

Platform services

Your operating system, Google Play and any share destination may process information under their own settings and policies. Sketchora does not control these independent services.

Contact and changes

For support, use the developer contact shown in the Sketchora Play Store listing or the Sketchora project repository. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
